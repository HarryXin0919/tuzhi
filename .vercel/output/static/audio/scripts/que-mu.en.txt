Nitrogen shortage: old leaves even yellow, nodules may still be pink. Molybdenum shortage: nodules go dull first.
Aluminium toxicity browns and bares the root tips; molybdenum shortage can keep the roots, but nodules do no work.
Don’t pour nitrogen on yellow beans as the first move; molybdenum is a trace — doubling hurts.
