Phosphorus shortage is dark green with purple; roots are not always brown and bare. Aluminium toxicity starts at the root tip.
Root burn lines the fertiliser furrow and you can see grains; aluminium toxicity is the whole acid layer.
Don’t pour urea on bald roots; don’t treat it as hunger without a test.
Agricultural lime: Follow a soil test. Commonly tens to about a hundred kg per mu. The aim is to drop aluminium, not to go alkali
