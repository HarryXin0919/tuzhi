After rain, maize is stuck under a crust.
The surface cracks, moisture runs, seedlings jam.
Clay also cracks in a drought; a rain cap is slurry then a skin.
Salty-alkali cracking often comes with white crust.
Don’t rotary-till into ribbons after rain; don’t smash dry crust into the seedlings.
Break the cap by cultivating: Right moisture, shallow loosen. Don’t wait until it bakes to brick
Name what you see before you feed.
If leaves, roots and the surface disagree, stop.
