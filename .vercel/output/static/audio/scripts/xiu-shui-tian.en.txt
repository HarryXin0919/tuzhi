Rusty water, sometimes an oil film, rice yellow and stalled, roots black.
Not a dryland maize story.
Nitrogen shortage still has white roots; ferrous toxicity has black roots and rusty water.
Waterlogging is no air; rusty-water fields often stack acid and ferrous iron.
Don’t treat rusty water as nitrogen hunger; don’t keep deep water without an airing.
Dry the field: Follow the growth stage and the station. Let the roots breathe
Name what you see before you feed.
If leaves, roots and the surface disagree, stop.
