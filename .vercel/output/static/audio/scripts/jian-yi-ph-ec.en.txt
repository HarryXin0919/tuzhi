Field pH paper and an EC meter are rulers, not verdicts.
You must make the paste properly.
A field kit is a quick check; a number still needs a lab send.
Soil colour does not replace pH and EC.
Don’t lime at the maximum from one paper colour; don’t trust a decimal on an uncalibrated meter.
Soil-to-water ratio: Follow the strip or meter note. Don’t mix it by hand on a whim
Name what you see before you feed.
