A white ring at the dripper burns roots; afternoon wilt, worse at the emitter.
More water-fertiliser makes it worse.
Too little drip is dry; too thick wilts more after a watering and shows salt frost.
Disease dies in patches; too-thick feed lines up along the drip tape.
Don’t chase strength; don’t fertilise the white ring.
