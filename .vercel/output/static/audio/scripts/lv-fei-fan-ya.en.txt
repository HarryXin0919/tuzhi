Fresh herbage in, seedlings yellow, soil hot.
Worse if late and thick.
Green manure stealing nitrogen is one kind of nitrogen shortage — microbes eat first.
A thick fresh-green layer can rot in spots; disease spreads.
Don’t sow thick on freshly turned green; don’t skip the nitrogen IOU.
When to turn it in: Mostly around full bloom. Too late, the stems lignify and won’t rot
