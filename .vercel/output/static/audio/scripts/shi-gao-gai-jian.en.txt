High pH, smears wet and sets hard dry, poor emergence — that looks sodic.
Gypsum is for alkali, not every white crust.
Salty soil is high EC, pH not always very high; alkali soil is high pH and poor structure.
Lime is for acid soil; lime on alkali soil makes it more alkali.
Don’t gypsum an acid soil; don’t high-rate gypsum without an outlet.
Agricultural gypsum: Follow a soil test. Commonly tens to a hundred-plus kg per mu. Must be tested; pair it with washing salt
Name what you see before you feed.
If leaves, roots and the surface disagree, stop.
