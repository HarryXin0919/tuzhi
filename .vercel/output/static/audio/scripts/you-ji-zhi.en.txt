Rub a handful: pale, dusty, will not clump.
Fertiliser vanishes after one rain.
Paler year by year usually means a thinner house.
One microbe bag will not rebuild it in a season.
Bare winters lose it fastest.
A neighbour that still clumps is a useful pair.
A gap greens up this season. A thin house leaks every year.
Packed is hard. Thin is no meat. A field can be both.
