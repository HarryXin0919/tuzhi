Sticky when wet, cloddy and cracked when dry, roots torn.
The tillage window is short.
Clay texture is born with the field; a plough pan is pressed in by rotary tillage.
Clay cracks wide and deep; sand rarely cracks like that.
Don’t rotary-till after rain; don’t smash dry clods and call it subsoiling.
Moisture for tillage: A handful that balls, then falls apart when dropped. That is the right moisture
Name what you see before you feed.
If leaves, roots and the surface disagree, stop.
