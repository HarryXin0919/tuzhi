Purple, short, few roots — not always ‘no phosphorus in the soil’.
Acid iron and aluminium nail P down.
Chill eases when it warms; acid-locked phosphorus stays purple after a warm spell.
Nitrogen shortage is yellow; phosphorus shortage is often purple-dark.
Don’t pour P as a meal without opening the lock; don’t heap lime and phosphate the same day.
Phosphate fertiliser: Band it from a soil test. Not “short, so double”. On acid soil, put it closer to the root
Name what you see before you feed.
If leaves, roots and the surface disagree, stop.
