Chloride-sensitive crops scorch at the margin.
Ask which chloride fertiliser was used.
Potato, tobacco, some fruit are touchy.
Potassium also scorches margins, but scorch right after potassium chloride is more chloride or salt.
Salt injury often whites the surface; chloride injury depends on the fertiliser kind.
Don’t use potassium chloride on chloride-sensitive crops; don’t treat every scorch as potassium lack.
Which potassium to use: Chloride-shy crops: potassium sulphate. Rate follows a soil test. Read the ingredients, not the spoken trade name
Name what you see before you feed.
If leaves, roots and the surface disagree, stop.
