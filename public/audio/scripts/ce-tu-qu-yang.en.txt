Soil taken badly poisons every later decision.
One hole, a manure heap, a field edge — none of those is the field.
Labs make mistakes; more often you took soil from the wrong spot.
Leaves can point a direction; they cannot set a rate.
Don’t dig one hole; don’t take soil from the manure pile; don’t mix pan and topsoil in one bag.
How deep to take soil: Dry crops: commonly 0–20 cm. Fruit trees: along the drip line of the crown, follow local advice
Name what you see before you feed.
If leaves, roots and the surface disagree, stop.
