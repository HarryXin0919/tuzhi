Patch death: look at the root first.
Black rot, knots, pinched stem bases are not the thin white roots of hunger.
Nutrient shortage is a sheet by leaf position; root rot is patches, roots first.
Drought is dry soil, the whole field wilting at noon; root rot is often still wet.
Don’t chase patch death with fertiliser; don’t drench a rotten root with strong feed.
This page does not hand out a bag: Stop the feed first and look at the roots. Sort it first, then open the matching guide
Name what you see before you feed.
If leaves, roots and the surface disagree, stop.
