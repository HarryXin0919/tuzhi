Old leaves yellow between green veins, from the bottom up.
That is not iron.
Iron lock yellows new leaves; magnesium yellows old leaves. Get the leaf position wrong and you feed the wrong bag.
Nitrogen shortage yellows the whole leaf more evenly; magnesium yellows between green veins.
Don’t treat old-leaf striping as iron; don’t pour more K; don’t dump magnesium sulphate without a test.
Foliar magnesium sulphate (first aid): Commonly 0.5–1%. A small patch first. Soil calcium-magnesium phosphate or magnesium sulphate: follow a soil test. When potassium is high, drop the potassium rate before you add magnesium
Name what you see before you feed.
If leaves, roots and the surface disagree, stop.
