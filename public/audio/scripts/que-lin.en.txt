Seedlings purple, dark green, stunted, few roots.
Warmth does not restart them.
Nitrogen shortage is yellow; phosphorus shortage is dark green with purple and a stall.
Chill can purple too and eases when it warms; phosphorus shortage still stalls after a warm spell, worse on acid land.
Don’t broadcast P on untested pH; don’t heap lime and phosphate the same day.
Phosphorus by the root (after shortage is confirmed): On acid soil, band calcium-magnesium phosphate. Seedling foliar potassium dihydrogen phosphate: labelled lower rate as first aid. Broadcasting the whole field is worse than putting it where the root can touch it
Name what you see before you feed.
If leaves, roots and the surface disagree, stop.
