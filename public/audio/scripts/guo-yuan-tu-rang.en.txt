Soil under the tree is hard, clean-cultivated, no cover.
Shoots short, fertiliser ‘evaporates’.
Yellowing is common, but the root may be clean tillage + high pH + thin organic matter.
Drought and thin often travel together; a cover can change both.
Don’t bake a bare tree row; don’t fertilise the walkway.
Organic manure: By tree age and local advice, in a trench. Don’t heap raw manure against the crown
Name what you see before you feed.
If leaves, roots and the surface disagree, stop.
