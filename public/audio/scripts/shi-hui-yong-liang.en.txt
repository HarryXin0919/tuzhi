You already decided to lime.
Now: which lime, how much, when.
Read the bag.
Agricultural lime has a calcium-oxide number; building lime is dirty and burns seedlings.
Gypsum lifts alkali, not acid. White powder is not one medicine.
No quicklime on seedlings; no builder’s lime as farm lime; no mix with urea the same day.
Agricultural lime (grain land, pH below 5.5): Commonly 50–150 kg per mu, twice. Sand: the lower end. Clay: a little more. Rates follow a soil test and the local agronomist. Better too little than too much
